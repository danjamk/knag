knag spike 110 — CodeMirror 6

One editing surface. Checkboxes are widgets over the bytes. Integrity checks are sticky red; capability checks stay grey until you actually do the thing.

Drills — run these on the phone

  1. Drag across four lines, including a checkbox row. Does it select?
  2. Long-press and use the handles to extend the selection.
  3. Copy it. Check what landed, below.
  4. Type a word with autocorrect and let it correct. Any red above?
  5. Dictate a sentence. Same question.
  6. Paste rich text from a web page. Watch the "no markup" check.
  7. Undo with the keyboard, or shake to undo.
  8. Tap a checkbox mid-line. Does the caret stay out of the marker?
